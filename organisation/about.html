Who We Are

Bringing hope, love, and opportunity to orphans.

Hope for Children is a non-profit dedicated to transforming the lives of orphaned and vulnerable children. With every hand extended and every heart that cares, we create safe spaces, provide education, and build brighter futures.

Since 2010 we have been walking alongside families in Nairobi and beyond — turning small acts of kindness into lasting change.
