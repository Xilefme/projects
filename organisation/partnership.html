Partnership Models

Multiple ways your organisation can get involved

No two partnerships look the same. We tailor every collaboration around your organisation's capacity, values, and goals — whether you're a small business or a large institution.

Financial Sponsorship

Sponsor a specific programme, a community centre, or a child's full care package. Full financial reporting and impact attribution included.

Staff Volunteering

Bring your team to one of our centres for a volunteer day — cooking, tutoring, painting, or building. Powerful team-building with genuine impact.

Cause-Related Fundraising

Pledge a portion of sales, launch an employee giving scheme, or run a fundraiser — we'll provide materials, branding support, and impact reporting.

In-Kind Donations

Donate goods, services, or professional expertise. Food, stationery, medical supplies, legal advice, IT support — we welcome all practical contributions.

Skills & Pro Bono

Offer your organisation's professional skills — accounting, legal, marketing, healthcare, engineering. We'll put them to work where they matter most.

Programme Co-Design

Work with our team to design and co-fund a new programme tailored to your sector and community — a legacy initiative that carries your name.
