Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our organization and how you can help.

How can I donate to your organization?

We accept donations through our secure online portal, bank transfers, or checks mailed to our headquarters. You can make a one-time donation or set up recurring monthly contributions. All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. Visit our donation page for more information.

What percentage of my donation goes directly to programs?

We're proud that 87% of every dollar donated goes directly to our programs helping children. Only 13% is used for administrative and fundraising expenses, which is well below the industry average. We publish annual financial reports for complete transparency.

How can I volunteer with your organization?

We offer various volunteer opportunities depending on your location and skills. These include mentoring children, organizing local fundraising events, providing professional services pro bono, and more. Please email volunteer@helpingchildren.org with your interests and location to learn about current opportunities.

Can I sponsor a specific child?

Yes! Our child sponsorship program connects donors with individual children in need. Sponsors provide monthly support that covers education, nutrition, healthcare, and other essentials for their sponsored child. You'll receive updates about your child's progress and can exchange letters. Learn more about sponsorship.

How do you ensure the safety of children in your programs?

Child protection is our highest priority. All staff and volunteers undergo rigorous background checks and child protection training. We have comprehensive safeguarding policies and a zero-tolerance approach to abuse. Our programs are designed to create safe environments where children can thrive without risk of harm.
