Why Volunteer

More than giving back — it gives back to you too

Volunteering with BrightRoots is a two-way journey. You bring your skills; you leave with purpose, connection, and the quiet confidence of someone who made a real difference.

Direct, visible impact

You'll see the child you helped eat, learn, or smile. No abstract metrics — just real moments that stay with you.

Skills & training provided

All volunteers receive free onboarding, safeguarding training, and access to ongoing workshops — skills you can use anywhere.

A genuine community

Join 340+ volunteers who support and inspire each other. Our volunteer network organises socials, events, and peer learning circles.

Reference & certification

Volunteer for 3+ months and receive a formal reference letter and digital certificate recognising your contribution and skills.

Flexible hours

We work around you. Weekday mornings, weekend afternoons, or even remote tasks — we'll find a role that fits your schedule.

Recognition & appreciation

Annual Volunteer Awards, monthly shout-outs, and an appreciation event every December. We see every hour you give.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience with children?
Not for most roles. We provide full training. What matters most is patience, reliability, and genuine care.
How many hours per week?
Most roles are 2–5 hours per week. We work around your schedule and never pressure you to over-commit.
What is the background check?
All child-contact volunteers undergo a standard DBS/CRB check. We cover the cost and process it for you.
Can I volunteer as a group or company?
Absolutely. Corporate volunteer days are welcome — contact us at hello@brightroots.org to arrange.

Have a question first?

Our volunteer coordinator, Mary, is happy to chat before you apply.
