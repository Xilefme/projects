Our Mission

To equip believers with sound Biblical teaching for faithful service, grounded in 2 Timothy 2:15: "Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who does not need to be ashamed and who correctly handles the word of truth."

Our Vision

A world transformed by Scripture-rooted disciples who live out Matthew 28:19-20, making disciples of all nations and teaching them to obey everything Christ commanded.

Our History

1995

Founded as a small Bible study group meeting in a local church basement.

2005

Officially incorporated as a Bible training center with our first full-time faculty member.

2015

Launched our online learning platform, reaching students in 30+ countries.

2023

Celebrated 1,000+ graduates serving in ministry worldwide.
